Privacy Policy – Tiro Al Palo

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Tiro Al Palo (package com.paragon.tiro) handles information.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Hosting): used to deliver articles. Firebase may process technical data such as IP address and device information to provide the service. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob: used to show ads. AdMob may collect the device advertising ID, IP address, and app usage data to show and measure ads, including personalized ads where you have given consent. How Google uses data in ads

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form and can choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

External links

Articles may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data security and retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data processed by third-party services is handled according to their policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
