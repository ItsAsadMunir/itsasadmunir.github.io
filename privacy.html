Push notifications

Push notifications are optional. On Android 13 and later the app asks for your permission first. You can turn them off at any time in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Tiro Al Palo → Notifications). The notification token stays with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging; it is not stored on our server.

Consent and ads

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) and can choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can change your choice later from the app menu, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process data based on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the app (content delivery, request logs, crash reports, poll votes, error reports), on your choice to receive push notifications and, for personalized ads, on your consent.

Data retention

  • Request logs at Cloudflare: kept for a few days (at most 7 days). Rate-limit counters (by IP address): about one minute.
  • Error reports ("Reportar error"): deleted after 12 months.
  • Poll votes: single votes are deleted 30 days after the poll closes; only the totals are kept.
  • Crash reports: kept for up to 90 days.
  • Data deleted from our database may remain in our database provider's point-in-time recovery (Cloudflare D1 Time Travel) for up to 30 days, after which it is gone.
  • Data on your device: kept until you use "Borrar mis datos", clear the app data or uninstall the app.

Delete your data

In the app, open the menu (Más) and tap "Borrar mis datos". This deletes, for this installation, your poll votes and error reports on our server, unsubscribes the app from push notification topics and removes the installation ID, favourite team and saved content from your device. The app then creates a new random installation ID.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, the right to data portability, the right to withdraw consent at any time, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Spain: Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es). You can delete your data directly in the app (see above) or email tiroalpaloinfo@gmail.com. Because the app has no accounts, we can only find data linked to an installation from that installation.

Older app versions (3.3 and earlier)

Versions 3.3 and earlier read articles and app content from Google Firebase Firestore and used Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity to protect that backend. Both have been removed from version 4.0, which uses our own API on Cloudflare instead.

International transfers

Cloudflare and Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

External links

Articles may contain links to external websites and apps (for example YouTube, X or where to watch a match). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites and apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Our server never stores the installation ID in plain form, only a hash of it. Data processed by Cloudflare and Google is protected according to their security practices.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
